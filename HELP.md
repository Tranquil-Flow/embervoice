# Help with Embervoice

## My Mac won't open it

Embervoice is a preview and isn't Apple-notarized. Download only from [this project's release page](https://github.com/Tranquil-Flow/embervoice/releases). After trying to open it, if you trust the download, go to **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway**, then confirm **Open**. Don't disable security protections. A managed Mac may require your administrator.

## The voice download stopped

Reopen Embervoice and resume from its setup screen. It keeps partially downloaded files. No account or separate model download is needed.

## My book won't load

Use an English or Chinese **EPUB** without copy protection. PDF and Kindle files aren't supported. The EPUB must contain selectable text and be under 50 MB.

## It's taking a while

A full book can take a long time. Try a preview first. The time estimate appears after the first new passage, or sooner if that voice already has measured timings. It is approximate, not a promise. The passage count increases when audio is saved; the current section is shown separately.

## Can I stop and come back?

Yes. Click **Stop this run**. Start the same book again with the same voice settings to reuse saved passages. Don't change the voice direction, voice clip or its transcript if you want to resume that version.

## I closed the browser

Click **Open Embervoice** in the small app window. Closing the browser doesn't stop generation; **Quit** does.

## How do I update?

Finish or stop the current run and quit Embervoice. [Download the new app](https://github.com/Tranquil-Flow/embervoice/releases/download/v0.1.1/Embervoice-mac.dmg) and drag it into Applications, replacing the old copy. Your saved books and voice setup remain on your Mac.

## Still stuck?

[Report a problem](https://github.com/Tranquil-Flow/embervoice/issues/new). Include your Mac model, macOS version and the message you saw. Don't upload private books or voice recordings.
