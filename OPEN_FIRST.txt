EMBERVOICE — START HERE

1. Drag Embervoice into Applications.
2. Open Applications and double-click Embervoice. Your browser opens.
3. Read the voice agreement in the app. If you accept, click Download voice model.
   This is a one-time ~4.6 GB download; leave room for your audiobooks too.
4. Add an EPUB, choose a narrator and try Generate preview.
   Then choose Generate full book. Finished books appear on Your shelf.

IF YOUR MAC BLOCKS IT
This preview isn't Apple-notarized. Only if you trust this project's download:
System Settings > Privacy & Security > Open Anyway, then confirm Open.
Never switch off Mac security. Work Mac? Your administrator may need to help.

Requires an Apple-chip Mac (M1 or newer), macOS 26+, and 16 GB memory or more.
English/Chinese EPUBs only. Personal/non-commercial use; respect book and voice rights.
No Terminal or extra software needed. Internet is needed for initial voice setup.

To exit, choose Quit in the small Embervoice window (not just the browser tab).
Updating? Quit the old app, then replace it in Applications. Saved books stay put.

Help: https://github.com/Tranquil-Flow/embervoice/blob/main/HELP.md
