# Embervoice

**Turn your ebooks into audiobooks, privately on your Mac.** Choose a narrator, try a preview, then make the whole book.

## [⬇ Download Embervoice for Mac](https://github.com/Tranquil-Flow/embervoice/releases/download/v0.1.1/Embervoice-mac.dmg)

Requires an **Apple-chip Mac (M1 or newer), macOS 26+ and 16 GB memory or more**. Check **Apple menu → About This Mac**. Windows and Intel Macs aren't supported.

## Start here

1. **Download** using the link above, then open the downloaded file.
2. **Drag Embervoice into Applications.** Open Applications and double-click Embervoice. Your browser opens.
3. **Set up the voice once.** Read the agreement; if you accept, click **Download voice model**. This needs internet and about **4.6 GB**, plus space for audiobooks. No account needed.
4. **Add your EPUB**, pick a narrator and click **Generate preview**. Happy with it? Click **Generate full book**. Finished books appear on **Your shelf**.

**No Terminal or extra software needed.** After voice setup, it works offline. Your books and recordings stay on your Mac.

### Mac won't open it?

This preview isn't Apple-notarized. If you trust this project's download, go to **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway**, then confirm **Open**. Don't disable Mac security. A work Mac may need your administrator's approval.

**Updating?** Quit the old app, then replace it in Applications. Saved books stay put. Use **Quit** in Embervoice's small window to exit—not just the browser tab.

English/Chinese EPUBs only, without copy protection. Personal/non-commercial narration only; respect book and voice rights.
