Your words guide delivery, but a prompt alone may not keep exactly the same voice throughout a long book.

Voice clip optional

Anchor the speaker with a clean 2–30 second recording (WAV, MP3, M4A or FLAC; up to 10 MB). Use only a voice you have permission to use. The clip stays on this computer. A style preset can then guide its delivery.

Microphone access is requested only when you press Record. Recording stops after 30 seconds or when you leave the page; nothing plays automatically.

The complete, matching transcript is required; changing the clip or these words starts a separate resumable voice version.
